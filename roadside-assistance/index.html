Frequently Asked Questions

What roadside services do you offer?

Jump starts, tire changes and fuel delivery, 24 hours a day. If the problem can't be fixed on the spot, we tow your vehicle.

How quickly can you get to my location?

Fast and reliable towing services. Arrival times depend on your location, traffic conditions, and truck availability. Our dispatcher gives you an estimated arrival time when you call.

Do you bring a spare tire?

We put on your own spare tire. If you don't have a spare or the wheel is damaged, we tow your vehicle to a tire shop.

How much fuel do you bring?

Enough fuel to get you to the nearest gas station so you can fill up.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Cash App and Zelle.
