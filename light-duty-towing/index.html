Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as light-duty towing?

Everyday vehicles: cars, SUVs, minivans, pickup trucks, motorcycles and other light-duty trucks.

How quickly can you get to my location?

Ordinarily, we aim to reach our customers within 15 to 45 minutes, depending on traffic and your specific location.

Can you tow a car that won't start?

Yes. Non-running vehicles are no problem. We load them onto the flatbed and strap them down.

Where can you take my vehicle?

To your mechanic, a dealer, a body or tire shop, your home or storage. We also handle long-distance tows.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Cash App and Zelle.
